Dashboard methodology

How the dashboard uses the 15 source-validated monthly reports from October 2024 through December 2025.

Source and scope

Every displayed value comes from the tracked SQLite database. Its nine recurring report datasets were extracted from official Town of Hingham monthly reports and validated across all 15 reporting months before load.

Definitions

  • Finished water: mean daily Finished Water MGD for the report month.
  • Accord Pond usage: total monthly Accord Pond usage in MG.
  • Accord Pond level: mean daily Accord Pond level in feet.
  • Complete coverage: one or more validated observations for each of the nine recurring datasets in a report month.

Analytical limits

  • The 15-month series is too short for reliable seasonal decomposition or forecasting.
  • Association does not establish causation; the dashboard labels correlations as descriptive.
  • Customer-service calls are an activity measure, not a measure of customer water demand.
  • Water loss cannot be calculated from the recurring report fields because distribution input and authorized consumption are not both available.
  • Source-reported estimated reads, corrections, and anomalies remain visible rather than being silently adjusted.

Reproducibility

The dashboard snapshot is generated by dashboard/build_dashboard_data.py. The complete exploratory methods, machine-readable results, and generated figures are maintained in docs/exploratory_analysis.md and analysis/output/ in the repository.
